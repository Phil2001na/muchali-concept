What the firm stands for

Established on four principles.

The firm describes itself as a humanitarian law firm. These are the causes it says it was built around.

  1. 1

    Against domestic violence

    Representation for people who need protection, and for the families affected.

  2. 2

    Against tribalism

    A practice that serves clients from every community in Namibia.

  3. 3

    Parental and children's rights

    Standing up for the rights of parents and children when the law is in dispute.

  4. 4

    Enforcing the Constitution

    Holding the state and others to the constitutional provisions that protect every person.
